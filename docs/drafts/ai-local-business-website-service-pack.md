## Pack summary

- Pack title: AI Local Business Website Preview Service
- One-line hook: Build one useful mobile homepage preview before you pitch the owner.
- Category: B2B Service
- Buyer: Owner-operated local service businesses with active operations and visibly weak mobile websites
- Problem: The business is credible, but its website makes it difficult to understand the offer, call, request a quote, or trust the next step
- Offer: A fixed-scope, mobile-first one-page website refresh built from verified business facts
- Startup cost: $0-$100, excluding optional hosting and domains
- Time to first sale: 1-3 weeks
- Difficulty: Medium
- Clear first action: Choose one niche and one suburb, find 20 active businesses, and document one visible mobile-site problem for each

This is a proof-first website service, not an agency fantasy. The operator finds an active local business with a clear mobile-site problem, builds one private homepage concept using verified public facts, and asks the owner whether they want to see it. The preview earns attention. The paid job covers proper intake, revisions, launch, and support.

## Who this is for

This pack suits someone who can use a coding agent or no-code site builder, check their own work, and communicate clearly with a local business owner. It is not suitable for someone who plans to publish copied business content, invent proof, or launch a replacement site without owner approval.

## Why they buy

The buyer does not need a lecture about design. They need customers to understand what the business does and take the next step on a phone.

Useful visible problems include:

- The phone number or quote action is buried
- Services are difficult to scan
- The site is broken or cramped on mobile
- The service area is unclear
- Trust signals are missing or hard to find
- The homepage has no clear next action
- Contact details conflict across pages
- The site is slow because of oversized media

Do not claim that a weak page is losing a known number of leads. Sell a clearer customer journey, a credible mobile presentation, and an easier quote action.

## Step-by-step launch plan

### 1. Pick one niche and one suburb

Choose a buyer lane you can understand and reach. A narrow list makes the research, page structure, and outreach more specific.

### 2. Build a 20-business prospect list

Use public business profiles and websites. Record:

- Business name
- Website URL
- Public profile URL
- Evidence the business is active
- One visible mobile-site problem
- Contact channel
- Preview priority

Do not collect private personal data or infer revenue, traffic, lead volume, ownership, or intent.

### 3. Choose the clearest opportunity

Select a business where the customer journey problem is obvious and fixable within one page. Avoid complex ecommerce, regulated claims, custom portals, advanced booking systems, and businesses with no reliable facts to use.

### 4. Create a verified source sheet

Before building, record every fact the preview may use:

- Business name
- Approved or public phone number
- Services
- Service area
- Opening hours
- Public review themes without copying customer names or long quotes
- Public credentials or licences only where clearly verified
- Source URL for each fact

Mark missing information as a placeholder. Do not invent years in business, qualifications, guarantees, prices, testimonials, locations, awards, or results.

### 5. Build the private preview

Create a clean mobile-first page with a strong headline, service summary, proof area, service area, call action, short quote form, and contact details. Add a private-preview label. Do not publish it on the client's domain or submit forms to the business.

### 6. Run the QA checklist

Check mobile width, headings, tap targets, contrast, keyboard navigation, image dimensions, form labels, telephone links, source accuracy, and placeholder visibility. Remove unsupported claims.

### 7. Send a specific preview message

Name one visible problem, explain that a private concept already exists, and ask permission to send the link. Do not insult the existing site or claim the business is losing sales.

### 8. Sell a defined launch scope

If the owner wants the preview, run a short discovery call. Confirm the buyer, services, service area, proof, contact flow, required integrations, revision limit, hosting, domain access, compliance review, and owner approvals.

### 9. Rebuild from approved inputs

Treat the private concept as a direction, not as the final source of truth. Replace public assumptions and placeholders with client-approved facts and assets.

### 10. Launch and offer care

Get final written approval, back up the current site, connect the approved domain, test the live form and links, and provide a handoff. Offer a narrow care plan with a clear monthly change allowance.

## Fulfilment SOP

### Intake

Collect the approved facts, assets, access method, success action, scope, and exclusions. Do not begin from a loose chat thread.

### Source control

Keep a source ledger for every claim, contact detail, service, credential, image, and proof element. Client-approved facts replace public research.

### Build

Start from the proven page structure, but rewrite the content for the actual business. Keep unapproved sections private and label placeholders.

### QA

Run content, mobile, accessibility, performance, form, metadata, analytics-preservation, and claim checks. Use a second human or a fresh review pass before launch.

### Approval

Send one review link with a checklist of facts that require approval. Record the approved version and the requested changes.

### Launch

Back up the existing site, connect the domain during an agreed window, test the production page, and keep a rollback path.

### Handoff

Provide account ownership, edit instructions, renewal dates, asset sources, form destinations, and the support boundary. Remove temporary credentials and access.

## AI prompts

### Prospect research prompt

Find 20 active [NICHE] businesses in [SUBURB OR SERVICE AREA]. Review only publicly accessible business profiles and websites. Return the business name, source URLs, evidence that it is active, and one clearly visible mobile-website problem such as a buried phone number, missing quote action, unclear services, weak proof, confusing service area, or broken mobile layout. Do not infer private performance, traffic, revenue, lead loss, ownership, or intent. Rank the opportunities by how clearly a private one-page redesign could improve the customer journey.

### Verified build prompt

Create a private mobile-first one-page website concept for [BUSINESS]. Use only the verified facts in the supplied source pack. Include a clear offer, service summary, trust section, service area, tap-to-call action, short quote form, and contact details. Mark missing photos or facts as placeholders. Do not invent services, qualifications, years in business, testimonials, guarantees, locations, prices, or results. Make the page fast, accessible, responsive, and easy to customise after the owner responds.

### Outreach prompt

Write a short message that names one visible problem on the current mobile site, explains that a private homepage concept has already been built, and asks whether the owner wants the preview link. Keep it specific, respectful, and under 70 words. Do not claim the current site is losing a known number of leads or promise improved rankings, revenue, or conversions.

## Website copy framework

- Headline: [Primary service] in [service area], with a clear reason to choose the business
- Subheading: Who the business helps, what it handles, and the next step
- Primary CTA: Call [number] or Request a quote
- Services: 3-6 approved service summaries
- Proof: Approved credentials, work examples, or short permissioned testimonials
- Service area: Approved suburbs or region
- Process: Enquire, confirm scope, book or receive quote
- FAQ: Only approved operational questions
- Contact: Short form, phone, hours, and response expectation

## Upsell and recurring revenue angle

Offer a care plan only when there is real ongoing work. Define the allowance, response time, and exclusions. Sensible recurring tasks include service updates, team or project photos, approved proof additions, seasonal hours, form checks, broken-link checks, and small copy changes.

Do not sell vague unlimited support. Larger pages, new integrations, rebrands, campaigns, SEO retainers, or urgent work need a separate quote.

## Risks and limitations

- Building unrequested public pages can create brand, copyright, privacy, and impersonation risk. Keep previews private.
- Public facts may be stale. Require client approval before launch.
- Review quotes, photographs, logos, and credentials need permission or a valid licence.
- Website copy may require legal or industry review.
- Forms create data-handling responsibilities. Collect only what is needed.
- Domain changes can interrupt email or the existing site. Use a backup and rollback plan.
- AI output can invent facts and break layouts. Verify every claim and test every page.
- A better page does not guarantee rankings, leads, conversion, or revenue.
- Some businesses need more than a one-page site. Say so when the scope is not suitable.
